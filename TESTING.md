# What to try

Open https://trader.redmesastudios.com on a phone.

## First minute (each era)

1. Tap an era. A card explains the goal in one line.
2. Tap "Show me how". A hand points at a cheap good, then the buy button, then the map,
   then where to sell. You should never be unsure what to tap.
3. The first trip has no pirates, bandits or storms, and the first sale makes a profit.
4. Hand the phone to someone who has not played. Note where they stall.

## A longer game

- Does a route that paid well stop paying after a few runs with a bigger ship?
- Do the news items (festival, glut, pirates gathering) change where you go?
- Is the debt a worry early and an afterthought later?
- Do you reach the retirement goal? How many days were left?
- Silk Road: did you run short of water? Did winter close a pass on you?
- Space: does the fuel range feel like a puzzle or a nuisance?

## Looks

- Each era should be unmistakable at a glance: paper and ink, madder red and sand tiles,
  black and cyan.
- Check large text (Settings, Display, Text Size) and a small phone.
- Add the page to the Home Screen and open it from there.

## Saves

- Close the tab mid-journey and reopen: the journey resumes.
- Ledger shows a save code. Enter it on another device from the title screen.
